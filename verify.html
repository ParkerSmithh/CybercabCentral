How verification works

Cybercab Central doesn't connect to any Tesla API. Instead, uploaded photos are analyzed entirely in your browser: file metadata is read locally to simulate plate/telemetry detection and produce a confidence score.

  • No image ever leaves your device
  • Confidence score of 85%+ earns a VERIFIED SIGHTING badge
  • Verified sightings count toward bounties and the leaderboard
